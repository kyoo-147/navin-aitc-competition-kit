# Round 2 Mandatory Rules

Source: https://docs.thucchien.ai/docs/round-2/regulations-and-technical-guidelines

Retrieved: 2026-10-03. Recheck before the event.

## FACT — room and participants

- Space must accommodate all 3 contestants, be quiet, sufficiently lit, and have broadband Internet.
- No fourth person may appear in the room or camera frame during the session.

## FACT — mandatory equipment

- 2 Internet-connected PCs/laptops. BTC monitoring records all screen activity throughout the session.
- 2 smartphones, each fixed on a tripod/holder and continuously powered.

### Phone 1 — primary recording

- Airplane mode; no network.
- Disable auto screen lock.
- Landscape orientation and BTC sample angle.
- MP4 or MOV, minimum Full HD 1920×1080.

### Phone 2 — live proctoring

- Internet enabled.
- Disable all notification display, auto screen lock, and portrait-orientation lock.
- Landscape orientation and BTC sample angle.
- Connect to the proctoring system using the code BTC supplies before the exam.

## FACT — recording

- Both phones remain fixed; no handheld recording.
- Cameras must not obstruct one another and must cover contestants, desks, and computer screens.
- Test about 30 seconds beforehand for sound, light, framing, and landscape orientation.
- Phone 1 filename format: `MãĐội_TênĐội_NgàyThi`.

## FACT — submission

- The team has 10 minutes after official work time to submit according to the challenge-specific instructions.
- Valid package includes the required result file(s) and Phone 1 recording.
- Video may be uploaded to Google Drive/OneDrive with download permission and sent through the official channel.
- Judging considers both the result and the monitored process.

## FACT — violations

External intervention, unauthorized assistance/material/tools, participant substitution, or similar misconduct may cause point deduction, rejection of work, or team disqualification.

## Operational consequence

The exact challenge package may tighten these rules. At start time, one member reads the live instructions aloud, another records requirements/checksum/path/deadline, and no implementation starts until the leader confirms the allowed-resource boundary.
