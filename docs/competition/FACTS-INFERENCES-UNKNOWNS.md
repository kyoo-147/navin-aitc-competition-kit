# Facts, Inferences, and Unknowns

Updated: 2026-10-03.

## FACT

- Round 2 is online and evaluates building an AI product using BTC-provided API/tools.
- The official technical page requires 3 contestants, 2 monitored computers, 2 fixed phones, continuous recording/live supervision, and a 10-minute post-work submission window.
- AI activity must be logged and sent to BTC; competition models must go through the BTC Gateway.
- Team API budget and rate limits are shared.

## INFERENCE

- A private harness can improve preparation and integration, but it is not automatically the official submission repository.
- Because the exact challenge is unknown, reusable adapters, verification, and deterministic staging are safer than pre-building a speculative product.
- Sequential member branches reduce collision risk on one shared machine.
