## Direction decision

The UX / Design / Experience Lavish artifact presents three directions. The current kit chooses **Utility**: high contrast, calm hierarchy, restrained surfaces, and explicit green/yellow/red evidence states. Design Studio records rejected directions and rationale in the same artifact; it does not create a third review surface.
