Failure and rollback

  1. Preflight detects missing prerequisites.
  2. Bootstrap snapshots only allowlisted files.
  3. Write to staging and validate syntax.
  4. Atomic replace of approved destinations.
  5. Doctor verifies versions, hashes and disabled browser attachment.
  6. Rollback restores the latest backup.

Explicit exclusions

  • No tokens, passwords or auth stores
  • No sessions, chats, history or memories
  • No browser profile/cookies/extensions
  • No daemon locks, PID files or caches
  • No machine-specific absolute paths in tracked templates
  • No overwrite of organizer hooks
